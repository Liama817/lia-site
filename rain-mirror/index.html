Rain Mirror

A fogged, rain-wet glass with you behind it. Use it the way you'd use a real window.

  • ≋Breathe toward the screen to fog the glass where your mouth is, or hold Space.
  • 🤏Pinch thumb and index finger to write. Open them to lift the pen.
  • ✊Make a fist and swipe to wipe a wide patch, like your sleeve.

Rain recording: “Rainy” by Ove Melaa (CC0).

Asks for your camera and microphone. No video, audio or data ever leaves this device; everything runs in your browser.
